# Data Deletion Instructions: @tul.builds comment replies

**Last updated:** 2026-09-24

**What changed (2026-09-24):** these pages moved to a shorter address, supiphat.github.io/legal. The old address, supiphat.github.io/tul-builds-legal, no longer works.

This page explains how to get your data deleted if you commented a keyword on an **@tul.builds** Instagram post and got an automated message and reply. The full picture is in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html).


---

## What we hold about you

At most, two things, in local files on the operator's own computer:

- the **comment ID** of the comment we replied to, and
- your **Instagram-scoped user ID** (a number Meta gives our account for you; not your username).

These are deleted automatically after **30 days** anyway. If you replied **STOP**, your Instagram-scoped user ID is also on a do-not-message list until you ask us to remove it or you comment a keyword again.

If you asked us by DM to delete your data, your Instagram-scoped user ID is also kept with that request until we've completed it.

We don't store your comment text or your username.

---

## How to ask for deletion

Send a request by either:

- **Email:** supiphatk17@gmail.com
- **Instagram DM:** @tul.builds

Tell us the Instagram username you commented from. Something like "Please delete my data" is enough.

The operator may use an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) to help handle your request. It never replies to you on its own.

---

## How we check it's you

We match the request to the Instagram account or username it's about. If you DM us from that account, that's usually all we need. If you email, we may ask you to confirm by sending a DM from the account.

---

## Timeframe

We complete deletion within **30 days** of your request. We remove your records from the local files.

---

## A note on the STOP list

If you replied STOP and ask us to delete everything, we'll ask whether you want your ID removed from the do-not-message list too. Removing it means we'd have no record that you said STOP, so the script would treat you like someone who never replied STOP. Keeping it means we won't message you again unless you comment a keyword again, which counts as a new request.

---

## What we can't delete, and how to delete it yourself

Some copies live on Instagram, not with us.

- **Your comment.** You can delete it yourself on the post in Instagram.
- **Our public reply to your comment.** We can delete it on request.
- **The private message conversation.** You can delete the conversation from your own Instagram inbox. We'll remove whatever else the platform lets us remove on our side.
- **Meta's own records.** Anything Meta keeps is governed by Instagram's Privacy Policy, not ours: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

---

## Confirmation

Once we've finished, we'll reply by the same channel you used (email or DM) to confirm your data has been deleted.

We keep the email or DM thread of your request, with our replies, for 12 months after we close it, then delete it from our Gmail or Instagram inbox.
